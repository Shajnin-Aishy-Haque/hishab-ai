Privacy Policy - Hishab AI

Welcome to Hishab AI (https://hishab-ai.pages.dev). Your privacy and data confidentiality are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how Hishab AI handles your information.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Hishab AI operates on a local-first, private architecture. All financial transactions, dhar khata records, balances, and categories are stored directly on your own device using your browser's private IndexedDB storage.

2. Google Identity & Drive Integration

When you choose to use "Sign in with Google", Hishab AI requests the following limited permissions:

  • Basic Profile & Email: To uniquely identify your workspace and personalize your profile.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): To automatically backup and restore your encrypted financial snapshots. Hishab AI only reads and writes files that Hishab AI itself creates (hishab_ai_backup_*.json) and has zero access to your other private Drive files, photos, or documents.

3. Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data to any external advertising or data brokers.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You maintain 100% control of your data. You can delete individual transactions, reset your workspace to scratch, or delete your account at any time directly within the application settings.
